# **App Name**: BusEmbarCAT

## Core Features:

- Vehicle Database: Maintain records of each vehicle, linking them to specific operators.
- Equipment Inventory: Track hardware components (validators, consoles, etc.) assigned to vehicles.
- Task Scheduler: Schedule maintenance tasks (quarterly, biannually, annually) for different equipment/vehicle types.
- Digital Checklists: Enable technicians to mark maintenance tasks as complete using digital checklists on a mobile app, incorporating details from section 3.1.1.2.1.
- Maintenance Log Summarization: Use a tool to process technician's maintenance log entries and suggest potential failure points. Incorporate the checklist from section 3.1.1.2.1.
- Incident Ticketing: Log and assign unplanned incidents reported by operators or the CMG, including SLA (service level agreement) tracking, up to a maximum of 3 working days for resolution.
- Inventory Tracking: Log movements of equipment, manage inventory stock levels for generic, specific 'free', and vendor-specific materials, linking each to a particular maintenance intervention and deducting them from current stock. Categorize material for reporting.

## Style Guidelines:

- Dark blue (corporate): used in the logo and main texts. Approximate value: #1E2A44.
- Light blue: present in icons and secondary buttons. Approximate value: #6A8FBF.
- Very light white and gray: neutral background, to give clarity and contrast. Approximate value: #F5F6F8. White: #FFFFFF.
- The typographic style is sans-serif, clean and modern. Use bold variants for titles and regular for texts. Lots of white space, clear hierarchy and legibility. Similar font recommendation: Montserrat, Lato or Open Sans.
- Minimalist, with plenty of air and well-separated sections. Centered on iconography: linear illustrations are used in blue. Rectangular buttons with slightly rounded corners. Top navigation with simple horizontal menu.
- Flat / linear style, with fine line in light blue. Use of circular pictograms to represent services (shield, gear, book, telephone, etc.). Human figures in simplified vector illustration (not realistic). Use icons from libraries such as Feather Icons, Heroicons or Material Icons in a linear style. Keep the circles around the icons for consistency.
- Base light blue color with text in dark blue or white. Includes arrow icon next to the text (reinforces the action). Hover probably slightly lightens/darkens the background.
